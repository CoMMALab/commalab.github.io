[Yitian](/members/yitian) and [Lucas](/members/lucas)'s work on simulating contact and actuation for soft growing robots has been accepted to [Soft Robotics](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/srb)!

The paper continues our collaboration here at Purdue with [Laura Blumenschein](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ME/People/ptProfile?resource_id=241064) and the [RAAD Lab](https://purdueraadlab.wixsite.com/website-1), where Yitian and Lucas share first authorship.
