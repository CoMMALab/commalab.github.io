Two CoMMA Lab papers have been accepted to [IEEE Transactions on Robotics](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/t-ro)!

Both continue our collaboration with the [Kavraki Lab](https://kavrakilab.org/) at [Rice University](https://www.rice.edu/), where [Zak](/members/kingston) completed his master's, doctoral, and postdoctoral studies.
[Thai Duong](https://thaipduong.github.io/) led the work on kinodynamic planning, and [Qingxi Meng](https://www.linkedin.com/in/qingxi-meng-0b733a125/) led the work on planning motion and perception together.
