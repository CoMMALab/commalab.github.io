Two CoMMA Lab papers have been accepted to [The International Journal of Robotics Research](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/ijr)!

The work on long-horizon POMDP planning is joint with [Hanna Kurniawati's group at ANU](https://rdl.cecs.anu.edu.au/) and the [Kavraki Lab](https://kavrakilab.org/) at [Rice University](https://www.rice.edu/), and extends [our ISRR 2024 paper](/publications#liang2024ropras) on accelerating POMDP solving with VAMP.
The work on soft growing robots continues our collaboration here at Purdue with [Laura Blumenschein](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ME/People/ptProfile?resource_id=241064) and the [RAAD Lab](https://purdueraadlab.wixsite.com/website-1).
