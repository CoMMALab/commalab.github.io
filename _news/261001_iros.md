## Workshops

Zak helped organize the [Workshop on Open Robotics Software](https://sites.google.com/rice.edu/iros26-robotics-software/), which discussed sustainable software practices for reproducible robotics research and the long-term challenges of maintaining widely used robotics libraries.

The lab also presented two workshop papers:

"Evaluating Barometric Tactile Sensor Designs in Simulation" with Bill Fan, Gilbert Chang, Jolie Lai, and Lillian Chin at the [Learning-based Robot Co-design: Generative and Iterative Approaches](https://sites.google.com/view/robot-codesign-iros) workshop, where Gilbert also gave a Young Researcher Talk.
